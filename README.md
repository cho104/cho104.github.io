### **About**

I’m Chioh, a sophomore CS student at Pusan National University in Busan, South Korea.

I’m most interested in how models learn without labels, specifically contrastive and self-supervised vision models (from CLIP to DINO), and knowledge distillation, where a student learns from a teacher or its own past self. The idea I keep coming back to is that you don’t necessarily need a bigger teacher, just the right asymmetry between what the teacher and student get to see.

I also love building and competing, from reaching USACO Platinum to winning 1st place in a multimodal AI challenge.

As vice president of PNU’s AI Developers Club, I lead the research group, give seminars, and organize competitions.

Away from my desk, you can find me restoring vintage keyboards, birdwatching, out on a 3-mile run, or making another McDonald’s run.

12. **Prosody-Aware Speech Representations for Emotion Recognition under Pragmatic Ambiguity** (Interspeech 2026)  
Co-authored with Yeonwoo Park (Hanyang University ERICA). Speech encoders such as Whisper are built for transcription, so they flatten the pitch and loudness that carry emotional intent. We injected pitch (F0) and energy into the shared hidden representation of a pretrained Whisper encoder. On a 59-class Korean emotion task this raised subset accuracy by 13.93%p, and on a new Pragmatic Ambiguity Resolution (PAR) benchmark of 1,000 Korean utterances, where the words alone don't settle the emotion, it gained 11.6%p and beat text-based GPT-4o mini.

17. **Provit: AI Document Authenticity Checker** (PNU × Upstage Document AI Challenge 2025)  
Built with one teammate as team Fried Pistachio. Provit checks whether an uploaded document has been forged, from a Streamlit web app or a Chrome extension. It looks at file hashes and EXIF metadata, image forensics (error level, noise and gradient analysis), OCR confidence from Upstage Document AI, and differences from PII-scrubbed templates of the same document type; Upstage's Solar LLM then weighs the evidence and explains its verdict.
